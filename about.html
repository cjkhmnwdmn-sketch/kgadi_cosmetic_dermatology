About Kgadi's

A cosmetic dermatology concept centred on aesthetic skin concerns.

The supplied business proposal presents Kgadi's Cosmetic Dermatology as a service focused mainly on aesthetic concerns affecting women. Its stated purpose is to offer non-surgical cosmetic treatments for skin and appearance concerns.

Business Vision

Confidence through personalised aesthetic care

The proposal places importance on how people view their appearance, especially their facial appearance. The website translates that idea into a clean, welcoming digital experience without adding services that are not listed in the source proposal.

“

Non-surgical aesthetic services for selected skin and appearance concerns.

Treatment Processes

How selected processes are described

01

Chemical Peelings

The proposal describes chemical peelings for hyperpigmentation, dead skin cells, melasma, acne, mild acne scars, fine lines and wrinkles, using acidic chemicals in different strengths.

02

Dermal Fillers

The proposal describes dermal fillers as injections used to reduce the appearance of facial wrinkles and lines and mentions hyaluronic acid and synthetic wrinkle fillers.

03

Periocular Peels

The proposal describes this peel for the sensitive eye area, including concerns such as crow's feet, dark circles and hyperpigmentation around the upper and lower lid.

04

Non-Invasive Fat Removal

The proposal describes this as an approach for stubborn fat pockets using cold or ultrasound waves to target fat cells.
